Underground Cave Gen

Any tag ending in "_layer" assigns this biome to that cave layer - it just needs to match a layer your addon (or the vanilla game) actually defines under cave_layers/. Tags are never namespaced/prefixed with your mod id in Cubyz (confirmed in the game's own src/tag.zig) - use a plain name, just pick something specific enough to avoid colliding with another addon's layer name. The listed options are vanilla's own layers; pick "Custom" to target your own addon-defined layer instead.

Biome Tags

Tags drive structure tables and cave-layer assignment. A cave biome needs a tag ending in _layer (also selectable below).
